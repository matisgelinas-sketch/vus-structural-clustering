07 — Honest caveats

Limitations

Small dataset
~28,000 variants vs. ~71 million for genome-scale tools like AlphaMissense
Uneven performance
Accuracy varies a great deal from gene to gene — not a single reliable number
Static structures
No protein flexibility, complexes, or bound states modeled
Not diagnostic
A hypothesis-generation tool throughout — every output is a candidate for further investigation, never a classification
